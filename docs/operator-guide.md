# Operator guide — MWI

Portal: Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets Authority
(PPDA, ppda.mw) -- Supplier Registration Portal exists per PPDA's own
navigation, with a concrete tiered registration-fee schedule published
in the Registration Fees for Suppliers Regulations, 2025 (Government
Notice 44 of 2025). Entity: Companies Registration and Intellectual
Property Centre (CRIPC) business/company registration (Companies Act,
2013 / Business Registration Act) + Malawi Revenue Authority (MRA)
Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN) record. Engagements seeking the
PPDA's own MSME procurement set-aside (Act No. 7 of 2025, s.62(11))
also clear the Participation by Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
Order, 2020's own eligibility gate (at least two of three declared
size-band criteria -- turnover / employees / assets -- for the
engagement's own declared category) before a filing can proceed.
Human-gated filing only.
