# Business model — MWI

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for Malawi.

- Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets Authority (PPDA)
  public procurement -- Act No. 7 of 2025 (PPDA's own site names this
  Act and its own PDF text was downloaded and confirmed genuine; the
  Act's own Section 1 short-title clause internally reads a different
  year, 2024, than its own Gazette/assent metadata, 2025 -- an honest,
  independently-corroborated discrepancy, see `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`)
- Companies Registration and Intellectual Property Centre (CRIPC --
  successor, effective 1 April 2026, to the former Registrar General's
  Department) business/company registration -- Companies Act, 2013
  (incorporated companies) or the Business Registration Act
  (sole-trader/partnership); own published fee schedule for sole
  proprietors (K10,000 without an agreement attached, K20,000 with one)
- Malawi Revenue Authority (MRA) Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN)
  registration -- established under the Malawi Revenue Authority Act,
  Chapter 39:07
- PPDA Act's own s.62(11) 60%/40% national-competitive-bidding Micro,
  Small and Medium Enterprise (MSME) procurement set-aside gate -- bars
  an engagement declaring it seeks the set-aside from proceeding unless
  its own declared size (turnover/employees/assets) independently
  satisfies at least two of the three size-band criteria for its own
  declared category, per the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public
  Assets (Participation by Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) Order,
  2020
